- `photo.jpg` — Your professional profile photo (square crop, min 200×200px)
- `cv.pdf` — Your CV/resume PDF

- Photo: replace the `SJ` text in the profile card with `<img src="assets/photo.jpg" alt="Saikrishna Jella">`
- CV: replace `YOUR_CV_LINK_HERE` with `assets/cv.pdf`
